Build the checklist the reader will use

The finished system tells a reader things like "72% of policies like this one say how long they keep your data; this one doesn't." To do that it needs a fixed list of the statements documents of a genre usually make. The computer grouped about 65,000 extracted sentences into groups of similar statements. Here you decide which groups become items on that list.

Step 2 · ItemsCheck the final list: is each definition clear enough to say yes or no for a new sentence? Fix anything marked "please check".
Step 3 · ChecklistsConfirm that each item of the experts' checklists (OPP-115, NeurIPS, ACL) is covered, or say why not.
