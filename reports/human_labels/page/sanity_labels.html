How to label (open once, then close)

For each section, mark every type of statement the section makes. This is the hand-labelled set that measures the teacher model (plan task 4.2): the teacher's answers are not shown here, so your labels stay independent.

  • Stated means the section contains an instance of the type, a narrower case of it, or a negative form of it ("does not sell data" counts for the selling type). Wording can differ from the examples.
  • Only mentioning the topic is not stating it. A section about cookies that never says what the site does with them does not state "uses cookies".
  • In papers, what the section says about other works counts only as what this paper does with them (for example "reviews prior work", "compares with other works").
  • Use Unsure when you cannot decide; it is counted apart, not as a yes.
  • The grey Closest by wording box is a rough reading aid from the embedder, not an answer. Go through every family below it.
  • Privacy: you judge all 58 types. These sections come from OPP-115, whose annotators labelled them in their own scheme; your labels also show how well that scheme maps onto these types.
  • Save needs the box "I went through every type" ticked. If nothing applies, tick "This section states none of these types".
  • Keys: ← and → move between sections; Ctrl+Enter saves and moves on (outside the note box).
